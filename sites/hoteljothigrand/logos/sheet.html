Hotel Jothi Grand · logo concepts

Each concept: main version, alternate colours, and the app / social-media icon.

1Grand Arch

A JG monogram under a gold arch with five stars. Matches the website’s midnight-and-gold look; feels premium and classic.

2Jothi Flame

“Jothi” means light. A temple-lamp flame whose inner curve forms a J. Warm, Indian, easy to recognise even small.

3The Facade

The hotel’s own building as a symbol: plum front, orange frame and the blue glass strip. Modern and instantly linked to the property.

4Royal Crest

A crown of three flames over an italic serif name, in maroon and gold. Elegant and traditional; suits signage, menus and bills.
